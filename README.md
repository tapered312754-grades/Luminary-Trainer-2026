## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Luminary**. This trainer provides optional assistance for Luminary's exploration, progression, resource gathering, and real-time combat systems. It is designed for solo play and should be disabled before entering online co-op sessions.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a relaxed solo experience, faster character and town progression, easier exploration, simplified resource gathering, or a more accessible approach to Luminary's combat and OpenZone objectives.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading into the game world and before beginning extended exploration. Movement assistance is intended to support Luminary's traversal-focused design, which includes climbing, swimming, gliding, and reaching hidden areas. Avoid activating movement-related options near ledges, water transitions, or loading boundaries.

### Tools & Inventory

Use resource and gold options before visiting town vendors or starting upgrade projects. Luminary uses gathered materials from activities such as mining and fishing to support equipment improvements and vendor development. Allow the inventory screen to refresh before purchasing, crafting, or transferring items.

### World & Environment

Luminary's OpenZone is structured around exploration, enemy camps, corrupted Towers, Life Trees, hidden treasure, and town-linked progression. Enable resource or god mode options before leaving town, then disable them when testing normal progression or preparing for co-op. Save after completing major objectives and town upgrades.

### Combat & Weapons

Luminary features real-time combat with melee, ranged, magic, blocking, dodging, and quick weapon switching. Use damage, health, and skill options selectively to assist with difficult camps or mini-bosses. Switch between available combat styles normally so the trainer does not interfere with weapon swapping, defensive timing, or encounter progression.
